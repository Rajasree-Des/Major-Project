LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
The 30 m thermal patch is treated as the HR reference for a synthetic SR experiment. This does not provide true higher-resolution thermal ground truth. The degradation operator defines the synthetic LR problem. Performance on synthetic degradation does not automatically prove real-world sub-30-m thermal reconstruction accuracy. Later evaluation should include synthetic benchmark performance and physical/thermal consistency checks.
Single scene, random patch split within the scene: overlapping neighbouring patches mean the validation patches are not independent of the training patches.
No training was performed in this evaluation.
